When substituting your own artwork for a host app's icon, preserve the features that make the component recognisable — its frame, its facets, its silhouette. "Simplify" means removing engraving and noise, not flattening to a silhouette.

**Why:** two corrections in a row on one icon set. A solid-fill star replaced a token that is faceted, lit and shaded, and read as a blot at 20px; a contract card was redrawn with only a plain rounded border and lost the ornate frame that made it read as a contract at all. Both times the correction asked for *more* detail, not less. What was genuinely worth dropping was the part that merged into a smudge at icon size — a gold ring around a gold tick, a heavy black disc behind a star — not the structure around it.

**How to apply:** look at the real component before drawing it, magnified — the facets were invisible at the reference's native size and only showed when the screenshot was blown up. Then check the drawing at its true display size, not at the size you drew it: rasterize large, downscale to the real size, magnify that, and see what survives. If detail does not survive, make the icon bigger before making it plainer — filling the box instead of sitting inside it bought back 28% and was what made a frame possible at 20px.

Sits beside [[feedback_minimal_ui_chrome]], which trims UI the app itself adds; this one is about not losing meaning when redrawing someone else's.
